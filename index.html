How I work

Three habits that carry across every team I have supported.

Start with the person

New hires stay when they feel settled. I pair training with follow-ups and support packs so nobody is left guessing in their first weeks.

Write it down

If a process lives in one person's head, it breaks. I turn it into an SOP, a tutorial, or a recorded training the whole team can use.

Report what matters

Weekly dashboards on resolution speed, activation, and engagement gave leadership numbers they could plan around.
